PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: July 29, 2026

At EliteDevelop, we value your privacy. We want to be completely upfront about what data we collect, how we use it, and how we keep our systems lean and secure.

1. Our Data Philosophy

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to advertisers or data brokers. We only collect the data necessary to run our core features, like your user profile, the forums, chats, AI sessions, and developer tools.

2. Information We Handle

A. Profile and Account Data

When you sign up, we save your username, name, email address, and a securely hidden (hashed) version of your password. If you upload a profile picture for the forums, our system automatically shrinks and compresses it to save server space.

Your username, first name, and profile picture are public on our user list. Your email and last name always stay completely hidden from the public.

B. Forums and Public Chats

Because our forums and public chats are open to the world, any messages, code, or topics you post there are public. Search engines can index them, and anyone on the internet can see them.

C. AI Assistant Sessions & Multi-User Chat

When you interact with the AI Assistant Hub, your prompts and chat history are securely stored in our database so that participants in your shared room can view the conversation history. Prompts are transmitted via secure APIs (such as OpenRouter) to generate model responses. Do not include sensitive credentials, personal identifying information, or API keys in your AI prompts.

D. Private Chats and Code Vaults

To protect your privacy, the text in your private chats and the snippets in your code vaults are encrypted. While they are stored on our servers so you can access them, we cannot read them. Only people holding the correct access code (for chats) or the direct link (for vaults) can unlock and read the content.

E. App Alerts and Notifications

If you choose to turn on push notifications or install our site as an app on your device, we save a secure digital token from your browser. This token is used only to send you a notification when you get a new message and your screen is locked.

F. Temporary Tool Usage

When you use tools like the QR Studio generator, your text and color choices are processed instantly in your device's active memory and sent right back to your screen. We never write this information to our hard drives or save it in our history logs.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

We use tiny text files called cookies and browser storage tokens to keep you logged in and save your settings. This includes:

  • Security & Login: Keeping you securely logged in as you move from page to page and stopping bad actors from hijacking your session.
  • Your Preferences: Saving simple choices like your dark mode or light mode settings directly on your own device, so our servers don't have to reload them every time.

4. Keeping and Deleting Data

We keep your account details and forum posts for as long as your account is active. You can contact our team at any time to request that your account be closed or your messages deleted. Keep in mind that when you delete your account, your public forum posts may remain online with your name removed (showing as "Anonymous") so that old coding discussions still make sense to people reading them later.
