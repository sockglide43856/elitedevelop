PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: September 29, 2026

At EliteDevelop, we value your privacy. We are fully committed to transparency regarding how your data is processed, shared, and protected across our developer tools and organization management systems.

1. Data Usage Principles

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party ad networks or brokers. Data is collected solely to power core features like developer profiles, collaboration tools, organization spaces, API key management, and AI sessions.

2. Information We Handle

A. Account, Profile & Organization Information

When you register, we collect your username, email address, and an encrypted hash of your password. When creating or joining an Organization, we store details such as organization name, description, logo, website, and your membership role (Owner, Admin, Member).

B. Apex AI Processing & OpenRouter

Interactions with Apex AI are passed through secure API connections via OpenRouter to evaluate prompts and return responses. Prompts and chat history are saved in our database to support multi-user shared sessions. Do not include passwords, private API keys, or personally identifiable data inside your Apex AI prompts.

C. Collabs, File Sharing & Documents

Files, documents, and code snippets uploaded or created through our Collabs features are stored on our servers so that authorized users with the shared link or access permission can retrieve them. File metadata (such as file size, upload time, and uploader user ID) is saved to prevent platform abuse and enforce security boundaries.

D. OAuth2 API Keys

When you generate an OAuth2 API Key, we generate and store an associated Client ID and Client Secret identifier bound to your user ID. We use these credentials solely to authenticate automated API requests made under your account.

E. Code Vaults & Encrypted Messages

Content stored inside Code Vaults and encrypted chat channels is obfuscated using standard encryption algorithms. Direct link handles or access passkeys are needed to decrypt and read these payloads.

3. Cookies and Local Credentials

We use session cookies and browser storage tokens exclusively for functional purposes, including:

  • Authentication: Keeping you logged into your account, verified organizations, and developer dashboard.
  • Preferences: Persisting settings such as theme options (dark/light mode) or interface configurations directly on your device.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain user account data, uploaded files, and organization records as long as your account remains active. You may request account deletion or content removal by contacting support. In instances where account deletion occurs, public forum contributions may remain archived anonymously ("Anonymous") to preserve technical discussion continuity.
